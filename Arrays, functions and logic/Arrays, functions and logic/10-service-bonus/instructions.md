# Task

A company decided to give a bonus of 5% to an employee if his/her years of service is more than 5 years.

- Ask the user for the salary and years of service.
- Write a program that calculates the bonus each employee gets, and prints the bonus amount and total salary amount after bonus.

## Expected Output

The output depends on the input. Examples:

```
Salary: 10000, Years of service: 6
Bonus: 500
Total salary after bonus: 10500

Salary: 10000, Years of service: 4
Bonus: 0
Total salary after bonus: 10000
```
