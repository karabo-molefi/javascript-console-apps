# Task

Write a program to find all the factors of 100. A factor is a number that divides into another number exactly, without leaving a remainder. For example, the factors of 20 are 1, 2, 4, 5 and 10.

## Expected Output

```
1
2
4
5
10
20
25
50
100
```
