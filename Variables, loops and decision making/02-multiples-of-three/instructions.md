# Task

Use a loop to display the numbers in the range 0…20 that are multiples of 3. A **multiple** is the product result of one number multiplied by another number. For example, the first five multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, 16 and 20.

## Expected Output

```
0
3
6
9
12
15
18
```
